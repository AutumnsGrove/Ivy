# 5f. Newsletters and the digest

## Purpose

Tame newsletters: a separate Reading feed (it exists), a **daily digest** of what is worth reading,
and **one-click unsubscribe**. A future idea, quiet auto-archive, is out of this chunk and must
never touch personal correspondence.

## Depends on

5a, 5b; `category` and `is_automated` from 5d. Uses 5e's summary machinery for digest text.

## Where we start

- `GET /reading` is real: the tag-driven Reading feed (3g), fed by the rule action "show in
  Reading". The digest part of that response is not generated by anything.
- **`List-Id`, `List-Unsubscribe` and `List-Unsubscribe-Post` are not parsed or stored** (checked
  2026-10-07; the raw message is stored, so existing mail can be re-derived). 5f.1 adds the parse and a
  mirror migration first.
- **Ivy makes no outbound fetches today.** It blocks remote content instead of fetching it, and the
  only SSRF guard is the connector's host check in `internal/accountsvc`. Unsubscribe is the first
  server-side fetch, so 5f builds a guarded HTTP client (private and loopback ranges refused after
  DNS resolution and on every redirect, timeouts, size bounds); the connector's check is a pattern
  to reuse, not a client to call.
- The Reading tab and the digest panel are in the design (`docs/design/canvas/`).

## Settled

- **Newsletters live in a separate Reading feed, with a daily digest and one-click unsubscribe**
  (`List-Unsubscribe`, executed **server-side with SSRF protection**) (PLAN.md 3).
- **Unsubscribing always needs a click** (brief invariant 6); an unsubscribe is never a rule action.
- **Catalog entries:** `digest_worthy` (skip, mention, headline), `same_topic` (different, same:
  dedupe and cluster items for the digest), `category` newsletter (5d). Digest text is produced by
  `complete()`.
- **Local signals first:** `List-Id`/`List-Unsubscribe` (once parsed, 5f.1) and the "show in Reading"
  rule are the free baseline; the LLM improves, it does not replace them.
- **Quiet auto-archive is a deferred idea,** and must never touch personal correspondence.
- **Summaries are plain text and never labelled as AI;** a wrong guess costs a digest line, not mail.

## Scope

1. **5f.1 Unsubscribe.** First parse and store `List-Id`, `List-Unsubscribe` (https URL, mailto) and
   `List-Unsubscribe-Post` (RFC 8058 one-click) with a mirror migration and a rederive of existing
   mail; build the guarded fetch client; a confirm step; the server-side POST with a timeout and size
   bound; the outcome shown. A `mailto:` unsubscribe is a message and therefore goes through
   the send queue and its confirmation.
2. **5f.2 Feed signals.** `digest_worthy` and `same_topic` over Reading items, cached by content key.
3. **5f.3 The digest.** Generation (a `Complete` over the selected items' clipped text), storage as
   a derived artifact keyed by day and item set, and the panel in the Reading view.
4. **5f.4 Settings.** Per-account on/off, when the digest builds, and what is included.

## Tests and exit

- Unsubscribe: one-click POST succeeds, fails (4xx/5xx), times out, redirects to a private address
  (SSRF refused), returns a huge body (bounded), and a second click is idempotent. Nothing is sent
  without the confirm.
- `digest_worthy` skip makes no digest call for that item; an account with the LLM off still gets the
  plain feed and a working unsubscribe, with no request to a provider.
- A personal message mis-categorised as a newsletter is never digested or archived (`is_automated`).
- **Exit:** the digest and unsubscribe work on the dev stack; the operator's live unsubscribe against
  a real list is their own pending line.

## Failure paths (STANDARDS 4a)

| Input | Bound | Above it |
|---|---|---|
| Unsubscribe fetch | timeout, response size, redirect count, SSRF guard | refused, shown, nothing retried silently |
| Items per digest | a stated maximum | the rest wait for the next digest |
| Digest input per item | clipped text | clipped |
| Digest build twice in a day | one artifact per day and item set | the stored one is returned, no second spend |

## Open questions

1. **Digest cadence and delivery:** daily at what hour (the potato's local time? per account?), and
   where it appears (top of Reading only, or a notification-free "digest ready" marker)?
2. **What is a newsletter:** `category` alone, or also `List-Id` present, and does the operator
   confirm a sender once?
3. **Unsubscribe UX:** a button per message and per sender, a bulk "all from this sender", and what
   it does after success (mark the sender, mute, tag)?
4. **`mailto:` unsubscribe:** acceptable to send through the queue with its undo window, or refuse
   and show the address?
5. **Digest as artifact or on demand:** stored (cheap to reopen) or generated when opened?
6. **Digest source:** headline items only, or every item `digest_worthy` says `mention`?
7. **Account scope:** one digest per account or one combined (a combined digest mixes accounts, which
   brief invariant 4 forbids for automated calls)?
